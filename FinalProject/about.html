Cat Altemus Artist Statement

The body of work I've created takes a look at emotions, ones that make us human. I guess I could say I got inspired by my own self being lost in chaos. I, for a while felt I didn't have a clear vision for my work, and where I wanted it to go. I had all these ideas, maybe one too many rattling around in my mind, trying to escape on to the canvas. And I did let one or two escape, eventually those that did morphed into something I could work with.

Losing myself helped mold this body of work into these pieces that ultimately reflected how I felt as a painter, student, and as a human. I incorporated bright colors to contradict the underlining emotion, to show that someone may seem fine on the outside. When in reality they are battling their own storm. Eventually working through the feelings of being lost, envy, breaking down and finally coming to an end. Where you can turn the page and start a new chapter within yourself.

As an artist I wanted to portray these subconsicous emotions with surrealism. My process of this was taking these feelings that I've felt and struggled with and start with what color comes to mind to the opposing emotion. Once the background color had been picked. I start to figure out the surrealistic part of the piece, and how I can portray this emotion we feel into a visual. The colors used for the emotions are ones I felt conveyed the message properly.
